<Lede>IPAbet is a fast and memorable keyboard for typing the International Phonetic Alphabet — a system-wide input method, with the same engine in any browser. Unshifted keys type their normal characters; the phonetic symbols live on Shift and Option.</Lede>

Where character pickers and escape schemes like [X-SAMPA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X-SAMPA) convert a transliteration after the fact, IPAbet emits genuine Unicode as you type. The notation is compositional — most symbols are a base letter plus a single modifier, <Combo keys="s ⇧H" out="ʃ"/> <Combo keys="t ⇧R" out="ʈ"/> <Combo keys="n ⇧G" out="ŋ"/> — and nearly every choice in it follows from five constraints.

## Five constraints

<Constraints>
<Constraint name="Identity preservation" tag="hard">
A Latin letter that is already an IPA symbol keeps its value, and unshifted keys stay native US: <Combo keys="s" out="s"/> <Combo keys="t" out="t"/> <Combo keys="m" out="m"/>.
</Constraint>
<Constraint name="Length bound — two characters" tag="hard">
No phonetic segment costs more than a base letter and at most one modifier: <Combo keys="t ⇧R" out="ʈ"/>. (Diacritics are a separate prefix, dead-key layer.) This bounds both the effort of any symbol and the amount of text the engine ever has to look at.
</Constraint>
<Constraint name="Local determinism" tag="hard">
Every segment is unambiguous and decidable from the characters right at the cursor — no lookahead, no backtracking, no global state. This is the property that lets the input method be _stateless_: it commits each keystroke instantly and rewrites the character before the cursor, the way Korean input methods do, because it never has to remember anything.
</Constraint>
<Constraint name="Phonetic operator algebra" tag="structural">
The remaining symbols are generated by applying a small set of operators to bases, with open-ended rules preferred over lists. Most operators are articulatory moves whose letter names the feature: <kbd>⇧R</kbd> retroflexes a coronal — <Combo keys="t ⇧R" out="ʈ"/> — <kbd>⇧J</kbd> palatalizes, <kbd>⇧W</kbd> labializes. <kbd>⇧H</kbd> is the orthographic h-digraph doing its several traditional jobs: <Combo keys="t ⇧H" out="θ"/> spirantizes, <Combo keys="s ⇧H" out="ʃ"/> hushes, and vowels lax (<Combo keys="i ⇧H" out="ɪ"/>). An operator applies wherever its move makes phonetic sense, which is what makes the system a grammar you can read rather than a table you memorize. Voicing is not an operator; it rides the base letter (<kbd>s</kbd>/<kbd>z</kbd>).
</Constraint>
<Constraint name="Convention reuse" tag="bias">
Among encodings that satisfy the rest, prefer the one users already know: pinyin (<Combo keys="s ⇧H" out="ʃ"/> <Combo keys="n ⇧G" out="ŋ"/>), the [Arabic chat alphabet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_chat_alphabet) (<Combo keys="2 ⇧H" out="ʔ"/> <Combo keys="7 ⇧H" out="ħ"/>), X-SAMPA (<Combo keys="4 ⇧H" out="ɾ"/>), and English spelling (<Combo keys="w ⇧H" out="ʍ"/>, from _wh_). This keeps it guessable rather than idiosyncratic.
</Constraint>
</Constraints>

## The rough edges

They are _predicted_, not hidden. A sound more than one articulatory step from any Latin anchor cannot be reached in two keys, so the voiced palatal fricative <Glyph>ʝ</Glyph> gets an approximate <Combo keys="g ⇧J"/> rather than a clean derivation — the length bound binding against a sparse corner of the alphabet. The constraints account for the compromises, not just the wins.

## Coverage

IPAbet covers every symbol on the standard IPA chart — consonants, vowels, diacritics, suprasegmentals, pulmonic and non-pulmonic — plus the complete extIPA 2015 diacritic set, the extensions for disordered speech. (extIPA’s symbol _letters_ — ʬ ʭ ʪ ʫ ʩ ꞎ ʞ — are not yet assigned.) Even the far corners follow the algebra: <Glyph>ɧ</Glyph>, the Swedish _sj_-sound, is <kbd>x</kbd> <kbd>⇧H</kbd> (⇧H is the hushing digraph, and the IPA defines ɧ as ʃ and x pronounced simultaneously); the tie bar has both placements, <kbd>⌥J</kbd> above (t͡s) and <kbd>⌥⇧J</kbd> below for when descenders collide (t͜s). The full mapping is on [the chart](/chart), in [machine-readable form](/keys), and as [raw JSON](/ipabet.json).
